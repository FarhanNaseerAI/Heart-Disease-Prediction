About Heart Disease

Why early risk insight matters

Cardiovascular disease remains a leading cause of mortality worldwide. Structured clinical signals — blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG patterns, and stress-test markers — can surface elevated risk before acute events.

Ischemic Signals

Exercise angina, ST depression, and ST slope changes can indicate reduced myocardial perfusion under stress.

Metabolic Drivers

Elevated cholesterol and fasting glucose amplify atherosclerotic burden and long-term event risk.

Clinical Action

Risk stratification supports timely referral, lifestyle intervention, and diagnostic workups.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is HeartVision AI a medical diagnosis?

No. It is an assistive risk model. Licensed clinicians must interpret results in clinical context.

Which model powers predictions?

A RandomForestClassifier tuned with GridSearchCV (n_estimators=300, max_depth=5), matching the research notebook.

Are inputs scaled before prediction?

No. The Random Forest was trained on raw features. Inference uses the identical unscaled feature vector.

Can I integrate via API?

Yes. Use POST /api/predict with JSON clinical fields and GET /api/health for readiness checks.

Is patient data stored permanently?

Predictions are kept in the browser session for report viewing and optional history. No long-term database is configured by default.
