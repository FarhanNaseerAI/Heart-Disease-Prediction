Model Information

How HeartVision AI was built

The production inference path is locked to Disease_Prediction_Model.ipynb. No retraining, re-fitting, or feature reordering occurs at runtime.

Dataset & Features

Clinical records from heart.csv with 13 predictors and binary target (0 no disease, 1 disease).

  1. age
  2. sex
  3. cp
  4. trestbps
  5. chol
  6. fbs
  7. restecg
  8. thalach
  9. exang
  10. oldpeak
  11. slope
  12. ca
  13. thal

Training Protocol

  • Stratified train/test split (80/20, random_state=42)
  • StandardScaler fit for LR/SVM experiments only
  • Tree ensembles trained on raw features
  • Random Forest selected as best model, then GridSearchCV tuned
  • Best params: n_estimators=300, max_depth=5, min_samples_leaf=4, min_samples_split=2
